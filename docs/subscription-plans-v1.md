# Hominode Subscription Plans V1

Status: Working product definition  
Plans: Essential / Plus / Pro

Subscription belongs to the community, not to an individual admin.

---

## 1. Hominode Essential

### Target customer

Small to medium communities that want to digitize their most important daily operations without paying for advanced automation or finance features.

### Main selling proposition

Core community operations in one place.

### Included capabilities

- Community management
- Building / tower management
- Unit / flat management
- Resident management
- Family members
- Resident vehicles
- Resident approval workflow
- Notices / announcements
- Documents / circulars
- Complaints / service requests
- Emergency / SOS
- Visitor pre-approval
- Visitor gate entry / exit
- Delivery / service-person access
- Visitor history
- Security staff access
- App push notifications
- Facility directory

### Billing & payment

Included:

- Maintenance billing
- Resident dues / outstanding bill view
- Receipt / payment-proof upload
- Admin payment verification
- Resident payment history
- Payment receipt record

Not included:

- Online payment gateway
- Automatic online-payment reconciliation

### Community bank accounts

- 1 community bank account
- Typical use: Collection Account

Bank-account governance is mandatory:

- Add/edit requires at least 3 levels of approval
- Approvers must come from distinct approved role/personality categories
- Immutable audit history must be maintained
- General users must not see running balance

### Governance & audit

Included:

- Mandatory security audit trail
- Bank-account approval history
- Basic ownership transfer

Not included:

- Advanced audit reports
- Custom multi-level workflow design

### Unit ownership transfer

Included:

- Basic owner handover
- Existing unit is preserved
- Historical ownership is retained
- Previous owner's private data must not automatically transfer to new owner

### Upgrade reasons

A community should consider Plus when it needs:

- Online payments
- More than one bank account
- Facility booking
- Parking
- Asset management
- Advanced audit reports
- Events / polls
- Community messaging
- Domestic staff management
- Marketplace

## 4. Shared Rules Across All Plans

### Bank-account governance

Every plan must support secure bank-account approval.

Minimum requirements:

- A proposer creates or edits bank-account details
- At least 3 approval levels are required
- Approvers come from approved distinct role/personality categories
- No single user can unilaterally activate sensitive bank changes
- Every proposal, approval, rejection and change is permanently auditable
- General users may view approved bank-account information
- General users must not see running balance

### Billing integrity

Maintenance billing is included in Essential.

Essential must support:

- bill creation
- resident dues
- payment-proof upload
- admin verification
- payment history

Plus and Pro add:

- online payment
- normal automatic reconciliation

### Security-critical audit

Security-critical audit logs are mandatory in all plans and are not restricted for monetization.
