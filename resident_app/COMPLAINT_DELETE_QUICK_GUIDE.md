# Complaint Delete - Quick Guide

## How to Delete a Complaint

### Method 1: Long Press (Recommended)
1. **Long press** on any complaint card
2. Bottom menu appears
3. Tap **"Delete Complaint"** (only shows for pending complaints)
4. Confirm deletion
5. Done! ✅

### Method 2: Via Detail Modal
1. **Tap** on complaint to open details
2. Look for **red trash icon** in header (only for pending)
3. Tap the trash icon
4. Confirm deletion
5. Done! ✅

## Important Rules

### ✅ Can Delete:
- Complaints with status **"Pending"**
- Your own complaints only
- After confirmation

### ❌ Cannot Delete:
- Complaints **"In Progress"** (staff already assigned)
- Complaints **"Completed"** (already resolved)
- Other users' complaints

## Why Can't I Delete?

**"Delete option not showing?"**
- Your complaint is probably "In Progress" or "Completed"
- Once staff is assigned, complaints cannot be deleted
- This protects the workflow and keeps records

**"Want to cancel a complaint in progress?"**
- Contact the assigned staff member
- Or wait for admin to close it
- Deletion is only for mistakes/duplicates

## Tips

💡 **Tip 1**: Delete only if created by mistake
💡 **Tip 2**: Can't undo after confirmation
💡 **Tip 3**: Long press is faster than opening modal
💡 **Tip 4**: Check status before trying to delete

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Delete option not appearing?**
A: Check complaint status - must be "Pending"

**Q: Deleted by accident?**
A: Cannot undo - create new complaint if needed

**Q: Error when deleting?**
A: Check internet connection and try again

**Q: Want to delete in-progress complaint?**
A: Not possible - contact admin or assigned staff

## Status Guide

| Status | Can Delete? | Why? |
|--------|-------------|------|
| 🟡 Pending | ✅ Yes | Not yet assigned |
| 🟠 In Progress | ❌ No | Staff working on it |
| 🟢 Completed | ❌ No | Historical record |

## Quick Actions

**Created duplicate?** → Delete the extra one (if pending)
**Wrong category?** → Delete and create new (if pending)
**Resolved yourself?** → Delete (if pending) or wait for completion
**Staff assigned?** → Cannot delete - let them complete it

## Summary

- **Long press** complaint card for quick delete
- **Only pending** complaints can be deleted
- **Confirmation required** - no undo
- **In progress/completed** cannot be deleted

That's it! Simple and safe. 🎉
