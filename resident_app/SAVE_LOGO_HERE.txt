SAVE YOUR LOGO HERE
===================

1. Right-click on the SECOND logo image (transparent/white version)
2. Save it as: logo.png
3. Place it in this folder: resident_app/assets/logo.png

The path should be:
D:\Resident_App\resident_app\assets\logo.png

Then run:
flutter pub get
flutter run

The splash screen will automatically use your logo!
